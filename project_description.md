# Plant Buddies: Project Overview

## Project Concept
Plant Buddies is a community platform designed specifically for plant enthusiasts, with a particular focus on beginners entering the plant care world.

## Core Objectives

1. **Knowledge Sharing**: Providing access to plant care tips and maintenance guidance for all experience levels
2. **Q&A Platform**: Creating a dedicated space where users can ask questions about plant care and receive helpful responses
3. **Growth Documentation**: Allowing users to document and share their plants' growth journeys over time
4. **Technique Exchange**: Facilitating the exchange of maintenance techniques and best practices between community members

## Target Audience
The platform is designed to be inclusive, welcoming plant lovers of all experience levels - from complete beginners seeking basic guidance to seasoned enthusiasts looking to share their expertise and learn new approaches.

## Community Focus
At its core, Plant Buddies aims to foster a supportive community where plant enthusiasts can connect, learn, and celebrate their plant parenting experiences together.
